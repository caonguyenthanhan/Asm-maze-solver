Execution Flow

flowchart TD
  A[Start] --> B[Mode Menu]
  B --> C[Speed Menu]
  C --> D[Display Menu]
  D --> E{Random or File}
  E -->|File| F[FILE_ReadMaze]
  E -->|Random| G[MAIN_GenerateRandomMaze]
  F --> H[MAIN_FindStart]
  G --> H
  H --> I[MAIN_DrawFullMaze]
  I --> J[MAIN_PrintLegend]
  J --> K[DFS_Solve]
  K --> L[Done]

DFS Loop (Simplified)

flowchart TD
  L0[stack_top == 0 ?] -->|yes| END[Return]
  L0 -->|no| POP[DFS_Pop -> (x,y)]
  POP --> CELL[Read maze cell]
  CELL -->|E| FOUND[Stop]
  CELL -->|Wall/Visited| L0
  CELL -->|Path/S| MARK[Mark visited 'V']
  MARK --> STATUS[Update status line]
  STATUS --> DRAW[Draw @ and maybe *]
  DRAW --> N1[Push neighbors R,D,L,U if valid]
  N1 --> L0
